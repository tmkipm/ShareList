Overview

MusicApp ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our iOS application.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Apple ID Information: When you sign in with Apple, we receive your name (if you choose to share it) and a unique identifier. We do not receive your email address unless you explicitly share it.
  • Profile Information: Display name, bio, and profile photo that you choose to add.
  • Playlists: Playlist names, descriptions, and song selections you create.
  • Social Content: Posts, comments, and interactions you create in the social feed.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Apple Music Data: With your permission, we access your Apple Music library to enable music playback and playlist creation. We do not store your Apple Music library data on our servers.
  • Usage Data: We may collect anonymous usage statistics to improve the app experience.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the app's functionality
  • To enable music playback and playlist features
  • To display your profile to other users (for public content)
  • To enable social features like following and sharing playlists
  • To improve and optimize the app

Data Storage

  • Your data is stored securely using Apple's CloudKit service
  • Private playlists and favorites are stored in your personal iCloud account
  • Public playlists and social content are stored in a shared CloudKit database
  • We do not have direct access to your private iCloud data

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal information to third parties. Your data may be shared in the following limited circumstances:

  • Public Content: Playlists and social posts you mark as public are visible to other users
  • Apple Services: We use Apple's services (CloudKit, MusicKit, Sign in with Apple) which are governed by Apple's privacy policy
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: View your personal data within the app
  • Correction: Update your profile information at any time
  • Deletion: Delete your account and associated data by signing out and contacting us
  • Control: Choose what content to make public or private

Third-Party Services

The app integrates with:

  • Apple Music: For music playback (requires Apple Music subscription)
  • Apple CloudKit: For data storage and sync
  • Sign in with Apple: For authentication

These services are governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Children's Privacy

MusicApp is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures including:

  • Secure authentication via Sign in with Apple
  • Encrypted data transmission
  • CloudKit's built-in security features

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy in the app.
